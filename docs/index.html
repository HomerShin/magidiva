Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email us.

Is MagiDiva really free?+

Yes. MagiDiva Community is 100% free and open-source under AGPLv3. No hidden fees, no trial period, no watermarks on exports. The Pro edition ($49 one-time) adds batch processing, folder drop, and gallery view for creators who need to process many images at once.

How is this different from Remove.bg or Adobe Express?+

Three big differences: privacy — your images never leave your machine; cost — those services charge per-image or monthly subscriptions; control — MagiDiva runs local AI via ONNX + DirectML, so you pick the model, the background, the format, and every setting. No internet needed after the first model download.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. After the initial model download (~20 MB), MagiDiva works completely offline. The AI runs locally on your GPU (or CPU fallback). Great for privacy-sensitive workplaces, travel, or no-internet environments.

What AI model does it use?+

MagiDiva uses BirefNet — a state-of-the-art background removal model that handles hair, fur, glass, and semi-transparent materials exceptionally well. We bundle three sizes: birefnet-general-lite (fast), birefnet-general (balanced), and birefnet-general-full (maximum quality).

Why Rust + Tauri instead of Electron?+

Smaller, faster, lighter. A Tauri app ships in ~8 MB vs. 150+ MB for an equivalent Electron app. The AI inference is native Rust (via ONNX Runtime bindings), so there's no Node.js overhead. And the security story is better — fewer attack surfaces, automatic sandboxing.

Can I use Pro features commercially?+

Yes. The Pro edition includes a commercial-use license. The Community edition is AGPLv3 — that means if you ship a modified version, you must release your changes under AGPLv3. For most commercial use cases (using MagiDiva as a tool, not embedding it), Pro is the right choice.

macOS and Linux — when?+

Tauri v2 supports all three platforms. We're prioritizing Windows first because DirectML gives the best GPU acceleration story. macOS (Metal) and Linux (CUDA/ROCm) builds are planned for Q1 2027 — follow us on GitHub to be notified.

What system specs do I need?+

Windows 10/11 64-bit, 4 GB RAM minimum, DirectX 12 GPU recommended (for DirectML acceleration). Works on CPU too, just slower. The app is ~30 MB, model files ~20–80 MB depending on which one you choose.
